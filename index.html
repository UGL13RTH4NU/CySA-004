Why I Built These

I built these materials while studying for the CompTIA CySA+ CS0-004 exam, particularly for areas where recognizing relationships, syntax, tools, processes, and indicators needed to become more automatic.

The interactive trainers are intended to create a kind of muscle memory: repeated active recall that helps correlate concepts and burn away the unfamiliarity that comes from learning material without years of SOC or vulnerability-analysis experience behind it.

Everything here is provided as personal study material. Verify information independently before relying on it for an exam or production environment.

About the Project

These are personal study materials created during preparation for CompTIA CySA+ CS0-004. They were developed using material from sources including CompTIA CertMaster, instructor-led training, Jason Dion's CySA+ course, Sybex material, and additional study work performed with AI tools.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by CompTIA. Study material can become outdated or contain errors, so information should be independently verified.
