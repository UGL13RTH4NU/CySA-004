OT / ICS Cheat Sheet

Big picture: OT, ICS, IIoT

OT monitors/controls physical processes. ICS is the industrial control subset used to automate and control industrial processes. IIoT connects industrial sensors/controllers/meters to internal or cloud IT systems, improving visibility and remote management while increasing attack surface.

Exam priority: OT emphasizes safety, availability/reliability, and process continuity. That changes how you patch, scan, contain, and shut systems down.

Do not over-separate them. All three overlap heavily in OT asset visibility and detection. If a question provides no unique product clue, “which one is better?” can be ambiguous.
OT-safe security decisions
  • Inventory first: old/unknown assets are common.
  • Prefer segmentation, least privilege, controlled vendor access, and continuous OT-aware monitoring.
  • Patching requires maintenance windows and operational coordination.
  • If patching is unsafe/unavailable, use compensating controls.
  • IR must include operators/engineers, safe shutdown procedures, and manual-operation contingencies.
  • Avoid assuming aggressive active scanning is safe on fragile legacy controllers.
