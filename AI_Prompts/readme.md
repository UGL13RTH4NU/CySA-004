# AI Prompts

These are base prompts I use to create content. I start with these and modify for output and quality as needed.


- For "textbook" flashcards I generally breakdown the PDF textbook into chapters/ sub-chapters and do maybe 10 pages or so at a time - it depends on the complexity of the material and how detailed I want the flashcards to come out.
- When doing "quiz" questions I just do 1 quiz at a time. Sometimes I preface the initial prompt with arguments if I disagreed with a keyed answer choice and/or answer choice explanations.
- For making the trainers it may very widely by what I'm looking to hone in on. I generally use the generic instructions and write out the different modifications for it by hand in Notepad.
