Common Exam Traps
7045 and 4697 can both describe service installation. 7045 is Service Control Manager in the System log; 4697 is a Security auditing event.
4624, 4698, and 7045 are not malicious by themselves. The account, source, command/path, timing, and surrounding events decide whether the activity is suspicious.
4732 means a member was added to a security-enabled local group. It does not itself say that the group was privileged.
4771 means Kerberos pre-authentication failed. Do not automatically call it Kerberoasting; service-ticket activity is centered on 4769, and attack identification requires pattern/context.
A burst of 4625 events can be attack activity, user error, stale credentials, or a broken service. Pattern + context matters.
Source & Scope Notes
The official CS0-004 objectives require logging concepts/EVTX awareness but do not publish a fixed Windows Event-ID memorization list. CertMaster directly demonstrates 4624/4625, and your course notes/Dion study specifically call out the ★ priority IDs. The + and Additional sets are clearly labeled supplementation rather than being presented as an official CompTIA list.
